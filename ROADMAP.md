# StatusLookout roadmap

Ideas for future releases. These features are **not implemented yet**; priorities may change based on feedback.

| Priority | Idea | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Service profiles: Work, Personal, Development | Switch between curated watchlists quickly. |
| 2 | Group related incident notifications | Avoid a flood of alerts when several products share one outage. |
| 3 | One-click portable updates | Install a verified download while preserving the executable path, startup and preferences. |
| 4 | Temporary alert snooze | Silence a service for 30 minutes or an hour without stopping monitoring. |
| 5 | Catalog component picker | Choose specific APIs, regions or products through the UI, where providers expose components. |
| 6 | Import / export preferences | Move a watchlist and its custom services to another PC. |
| 7 | Incident detail view and CSV export | Browse provider incident links and share the locally recorded history. |
| 8 | Follow the Windows theme | Automatically use the system's light or dark setting. |

Suggest a feature or discuss a priority through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Bouneh/statuslookout/issues). Provider-published status remains the source of truth; plans should not promise measured uptime or incident durations that the available data cannot support.
